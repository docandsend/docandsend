1. Executive Summary

This session addressed critical local SEO deficiencies for docandsend.com, a mobile notary service in Nashville, TN. The site had a catastrophic single-page architecture (only 1 URL indexed by Google) despite serving 4+ geographic areas. Two major issues were identified and resolved via pull requests:

  1. Site Architecture Collapse — Expanded from 1 page to 9 pages with polycentric location targeting
  2. Brand Entity Confusion — Google conflated "Doc and Send" with Dropbox's "DocSend" SaaS product; resolved via schema, meta, and content disambiguation
