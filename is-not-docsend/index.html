Who We Are Not

DocSend (docsend.com) is a separate company owned by Dropbox that provides virtual data rooms for secure document sharing, fundraising, and sales enablement. DocSend serves technology companies, investment firms, and sales teams—primarily in software and finance.

DocAndSend has no affiliation with DocSend or Dropbox. We are an independent local service business in Tennessee. We do not offer software, subscriptions, file sharing, or virtual data rooms of any kind.
