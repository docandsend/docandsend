Tennessee Notary Commission

I am commissioned as a Notary Public by the State of Tennessee, authorized to perform notarial acts throughout Tennessee. My commission ensures that every signature I witness, every document I notarize, and every seal I apply meets the legal standards required by state law.

I take this responsibility seriously. Notarization is more than stamping a document—it's about verifying identity, ensuring understanding, and protecting all parties from fraud or coercion. My job is to make sure your documents are executed properly the first time, so they hold up when it matters most.

Licensed Realtor in Tennessee

In addition to my notary commission, I am also a Licensed Realtor in Tennessee. This means I have a deep understanding of real estate transactions, from purchase agreements and closing documents to property deeds, title transfers, and refinance paperwork.

If you're buying or selling a home, refinancing your mortgage, or handling any real estate closing, you can trust that I know what I'm looking at. I understand the urgency, the complexity, and the stakes involved in real estate deals—and I'm here to help ensure your signing goes smoothly.

Bonded & Insured for Your Protection

As a professional mobile notary, I carry a notary bond and Errors & Omissions (E&O) insurance. This provides you with an additional layer of protection and peace of mind. If any error were to occur in the notarization process, you're covered.
