How Real Estate Closing Notarization Works

Simple, professional, and stress-free

1
Schedule Your Closing

Contact us by phone (615-484-2552) or online. Provide your closing date, location preference, and number of signers. We coordinate with your title company or lender as needed.

2
We Come to You

Lisa arrives at your chosen location with all notary supplies. She reviews documents, verifies identification, explains each signature, and ensures everything is executed correctly.

3
Documents Returned Promptly

Notarized documents are scanned and emailed immediately (if required), then shipped overnight or hand-delivered to your title company, lender, or attorney to keep your closing on schedule.
