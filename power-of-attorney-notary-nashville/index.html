What is a Power of Attorney?

Understanding your POA options

A Power of Attorney (POA) is a legal document that grants someone you trust the authority to act on your behalf in financial, legal, healthcare, or personal matters. This authorized person, called your "agent" or "attorney-in-fact," can make decisions and sign documents when you're unable to do so yourself.

POA documents require proper notarization to be legally valid in Tennessee. As a mobile notary with extensive healthcare and legal document experience, I ensure your Power of Attorney is executed correctly, with proper witnessing and notarization that meets all state requirements.

Types of Power of Attorney

General Power of Attorney

Grants broad authority to manage your financial and legal affairs. Typically used for temporary situations like extended travel or when you're unavailable. This POA automatically ends if you become incapacitated.

When You Need POA Notarization

Estate Planning

Establish authority for family members or trusted individuals to manage your affairs if you become unable. Essential component of comprehensive estate planning alongside wills and healthcare directives.

Aging Parent Care

Adult children often need POA to help aging parents with financial decisions, healthcare choices, and daily management. Critical for Alzheimer's, dementia, or declining health situations.

Military Deployment

Service members deploying overseas need someone to handle financial and legal matters back home. General or durable POA provides peace of mind during deployment.

Real Estate Transactions

When you can't be present for a closing or property sale, POA allows your agent to sign documents and complete the transaction. As a Licensed Realtor, I understand these requirements thoroughly.

Business Operations

Business owners traveling or managing multiple ventures use POA to authorize others to handle contracts, banking, and operational decisions in their absence.

Hospital & Medical Needs

Medical emergencies or planned surgeries often require healthcare POA. With 25 years of healthcare experience, I can bring notary services directly to hospitals, rehab facilities, or nursing homes.
